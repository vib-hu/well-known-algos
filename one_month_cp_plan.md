## **Tips for Success**

1. **Consistency**: Study every day, even if just 1 hour
2. **Active Learning**: Implement algorithms from scratch
3. **Practice Contests**: Participate in 2-3 contests per week
4. **Review**: Spend time understanding solutions you couldn't solve
5. **Time Management**: Practice solving problems under time pressure
6. **Communication**: Practice explaining your approach clearly
7. **Debugging**: Learn to debug efficiently
8. **Patterns**: Focus on recognizing problem patterns quickly

## **Adjustment Guidelines**

- If you're struggling with a topic, spend an extra day on it
- If you're ahead of schedule, add more challenging problems
- Adjust daily hours based on your schedule and energy levels
- Focus more on FAANG prep if interviews are priority, or CP if contests are the goal

**Remember:** This is an intensive plan. Adjust the pace based on your current level and available time. The key is consistent daily practice and gradual improvement!
